# Vickyvaio Breakouts 🚀

An institutional-grade Indian Stock & F&O Momentum Breakout Scanner inspired by **CA Arvind Mangal (`#Chart_Sab_Kuch_Bolta_Hai`)** and **Option Edge Tamil (High-Beta F&O Momentum)**.

[![Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/button)](https://vercel.com/new/clone?repository-url=https://github.com/vigneshrece92-cmyk/pro-stock-fno-scanner)

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Full NSE Derivatives (F&O) Coverage:**
   - Monitors the entire active universe of NSE F&O contracts across all 10 major sectors:
     - **Defense & PSU** (`BEL`, `HAL`, `BHEL`, `COALINDIA`, `NTPC`, `RECLTD`, etc.)
     - **Banking & Financials** (`HDFCBANK`, `ICICIBANK`, `SBIN`, `BAJFINANCE`, etc.)
     - **Auto & Ancillaries** (`TATAMOTORS`, `MARUTI`, `M&M`, `BAJAJ-AUTO`, etc.)
     - **Metals & Mining** (`TATASTEEL`, `JSWSTEEL`, `HINDALCO`, `VEDL`, etc.)
     - **IT & Technology** (`TCS`, `INFY`, `HCLTECH`, `COFORGE`, `PERSISTENT`, etc.)
     - **Pharma & Healthcare** (`SUNPHARMA`, `CIPLA`, `DIVISLAB`, `LUPIN`, etc.)
     - **Consumer & Retail** (`TRENT`, `TITAN`, `ITC`, `DIXON`, `POLYCAB`, etc.)
     - **Energy & Telecom** (`RELIANCE`, `POWERGRID`, `BHARTIARTL`, `TATAPOWER`, etc.)
     - **Realty & Infrastructure** (`DLF`, `GODREJPROP`, `LT`, `SIEMENS`, `ABB`, etc.)
     - **Chemicals & Fertilizers** (`SRF`, `PIDILITIND`, `DEEPAKNTR`, etc.)

2. **Categorized Breakout Horizons:**
   - **Monthly Holding (Arvind Mangal Base):** Multi-month / 52-week consolidation breakouts with institutional volume accumulation.
   - **Weekly Holding:** Medium-term consolidation range breakouts.
   - **Daily Fast F&O (Option Edge Tamil):** High-beta momentum surges with RVOL $\ge 1.4\times$ to $2.8\times$.

3. **Complete Execution Package:**
   - **Cash/Stock Trade:** Entry Price, Stop Loss, Target 1, Target 2, R:R ratio.
   - **F&O Option Call:** Recommended ATM Strike (e.g. `BEL 310 CE`), Option Entry Premium, Option SL, Option Target 1 (+45%), Option Target 2 (+95% Doubler).

4. **Interactive TradingView Candlestick Charts:**
   - Full-screen live candlestick modal with pre-loaded **20 EMA**, **50 EMA**, **Volume**, and **RSI**.
